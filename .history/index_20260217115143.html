Smile Designing

Customized cosmetic enhancement for perfect smiles

Smile designing is a cosmetic dental procedure that focuses on enhancing the appearance of a person’s smile through customized treatment planning. It involves analyzing facial features, tooth shape, size, color, alignment, and gum structure to create a balanced and attractive smile. Treatments may include teeth whitening, veneers, braces, aligners, gum contouring, or crowns, depending on the patient’s needs. Smile designing combines dental science with artistic precision to ensure natural-looking results. The goal is not only to improve aesthetics but also to maintain proper function and oral health. With personalized planning, smile designing helps boost confidence and create a harmonious, beautiful smile.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional plaque and tartar removal

Teeth cleaning, also known as professional dental scaling and polishing, is a preventive procedure that removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains from the teeth. Even with regular brushing and flossing, some deposits can build up over time, leading to cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. During the procedure, a dentist or hygienist carefully cleans around the teeth and gum line to maintain healthy gums and prevent infection. Polishing helps smooth the tooth surface and restore natural shine. Regular teeth cleaning, usually recommended every six months, plays a vital role in maintaining oral hygiene, preventing dental problems, and keeping your smile fresh, bright, and healthy.

Root Canal Treatment

Pain relief and infected tooth treatment

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) is a dental procedure used to treat infected or severely decayed teeth while preserving the natural tooth structure. When the inner pulp of the tooth becomes infected due to deep cavities, cracks, or trauma, it can cause severe pain and swelling. During RCT, the dentist carefully removes the infected pulp, cleans and disinfects the root canals, and then fills and seals them to prevent further infection. Finally, a crown is usually placed to restore strength and function. RCT helps eliminate pain, save the natural tooth, and restore normal chewing ability, ensuring long-term oral health and comfort.

Emergency Care

Immediate treatment for urgent dental issues

Emergency dental care provides immediate treatment for urgent oral health problems that require quick attention to relieve pain and prevent further damage. It includes managing severe toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, swelling, infections, bleeding gums, and dental trauma caused by accidents. Prompt treatment can save a natural tooth and stop complications from spreading. The dentist first diagnoses the issue, controls pain, and stabilizes the condition before recommending further treatment if needed. Emergency care ensures patient comfort, prevents worsening of the problem, and protects overall oral health. Quick professional intervention can make a significant difference in preserving your smile and avoiding long-term dental issues.

Tooth Extraction

Safe removal of damaged teeth

Tooth extraction is a dental procedure performed to remove a damaged, decayed, or problematic tooth that cannot be saved through other treatments. It may be necessary in cases of severe infection, advanced gum disease, overcrowding, or impacted wisdom teeth. The procedure is carried out under local anesthesia to ensure comfort and minimal pain. After removal, the dentist provides proper care instructions to promote healing and prevent complications. In many cases, replacement options such as dental implants, bridges, or partial dentures are recommended to restore function and maintain proper alignment. Tooth extraction helps relieve pain, prevent infection spread, and protect overall oral health.

Crown & Bridge

Restore strength and replace missing teeth

Crowns and bridges are restorative dental treatments used to repair and replace damaged or missing teeth. A dental crown is a custom-made cap placed over a weakened or broken tooth to restore its shape, strength, and appearance. It protects the tooth after procedures like root canal treatment or large fillings. A dental bridge, on the other hand, is used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to the neighboring natural teeth or implants. Both crowns and bridges are designed to look natural and blend seamlessly with your smile. They help restore proper chewing function, maintain alignment, and improve overall oral health and confidence.

Dentures

Removable replacement for missing teeth

Dentures are removable dental appliances used to replace missing teeth and restore oral function and appearance. They are custom-made to fit comfortably over the gums and can replace a few missing teeth (partial dentures) or an entire arch (complete dentures). Dentures help improve chewing ability, speech clarity, and facial structure by supporting the lips and cheeks. Modern dentures are designed to look natural and provide a comfortable fit. Proper care and regular dental check-ups are essential to maintain their durability and hygiene. Dentures offer an affordable and effective solution for restoring confidence, functionality, and a complete, healthy-looking smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common dental queries

How long does orthodontic treatment with braces take? +
The duration of orthodontic treatment typically ranges from 12 to 24 months, depending on the complexity of your case. Invisible braces (clear aligners) may take 6-18 months for mild to moderate cases. During your consultation, our orthodontists will provide a personalized timeline based on your specific needs and goals.
Is root canal treatment painful? +
Modern root canal treatments are virtually painless! We use advanced local anesthesia and gentle techniques to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. Most patients report that root canal therapy is no more uncomfortable than getting a regular filling. We also offer sedation options for anxious patients.
What makes dental implants better than dentures? +
Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that fuse with your jawbone, providing stability, natural appearance, and functionality similar to real teeth. Unlike dentures, implants don't slip, require no special maintenance beyond regular brushing, preserve jawbone health, and can last a lifetime with proper care. They're the closest thing to natural teeth.
How often should I get my teeth professionally cleaned? +
We recommend professional teeth cleaning every 6 months for most patients. However, if you have gum disease, heavy plaque buildup, or other oral health concerns, you may benefit from more frequent cleanings (every 3-4 months). Regular cleanings prevent cavities, gum disease, and keep your smile bright and healthy.
