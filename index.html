Orthodontic Care

Comprehensive care for overall oral health

We provide comprehensive orthodontic care for children, teens, and adults, including braces, clear aligners, and correction of crowded or misaligned teeth. Our clinic has an in-house orthodontic specialist available on scheduled days, ensuring smooth appointment planning and faster treatment progress. With no external referrals required, patients enjoy convenient scheduling, consistent follow-ups, and personalized care under one roof.

Smile Designing

Customized cosmetic enhancement for perfect smiles

Smile designing is a cosmetic dental procedure that focuses on enhancing the appearance of a person's smile through customized treatment planning. It involves analyzing facial features, tooth shape, size, color, alignment, and gum structure to create a balanced and attractive smile. Treatments may include teeth whitening, veneers, braces, aligners, gum contouring, or crowns.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional plaque and tartar removal

Teeth cleaning, also known as professional dental scaling and polishing, is a preventive procedure that removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains from the teeth. Regular teeth cleaning, usually recommended every six months, plays a vital role in maintaining oral hygiene, preventing dental problems, and keeping your smile fresh, bright, and healthy.

Root Canal Treatment

Pain relief and infected tooth treatment

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) is a dental procedure used to treat infected or severely decayed teeth while preserving the natural tooth structure. During RCT, the dentist carefully removes the infected pulp, cleans and disinfects the root canals, and then fills and seals them. A crown is usually placed to restore strength and function.

Emergency Care

Immediate treatment for urgent dental issues

Emergency dental care provides immediate treatment for urgent oral health problems. It includes managing severe toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, swelling, infections, bleeding gums, and dental trauma caused by accidents. Quick professional intervention can make a significant difference in preserving your smile.

Tooth Extraction

Safe removal of damaged teeth

Tooth extraction is a dental procedure performed to remove a damaged, decayed, or problematic tooth. It may be necessary in cases of severe infection, advanced gum disease, overcrowding, or impacted wisdom teeth. The procedure is carried out under local anesthesia to ensure comfort and minimal pain.

Crown & Bridge

Restore strength and replace missing teeth

Crowns and bridges are restorative dental treatments used to repair and replace damaged or missing teeth. A dental crown is a custom-made cap placed over a weakened tooth. A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to neighboring natural teeth or implants.

Dentures

Removable replacement for missing teeth

Dentures are removable dental appliances used to replace missing teeth and restore oral function. They can replace a few missing teeth (partial dentures) or an entire arch (complete dentures). Modern dentures are designed to look natural and provide a comfortable fit, offering an affordable solution for restoring confidence.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common dental queries

How long does orthodontic treatment with braces take? +
The duration of orthodontic treatment typically ranges from 12 to 24 months, depending on the complexity of your case. Invisible braces (clear aligners) may take 6-18 months for mild to moderate cases. During your consultation, our orthodontists will provide a personalized timeline based on your specific needs and goals.
Is root canal treatment painful? +
Modern root canal treatments are virtually painless! We use advanced local anesthesia and gentle techniques to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. Most patients report that root canal therapy is no more uncomfortable than getting a regular filling. We also offer sedation options for anxious patients.
What makes dental implants better than dentures? +
Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that fuse with your jawbone, providing stability, natural appearance, and functionality similar to real teeth. Unlike dentures, implants don't slip, require no special maintenance beyond regular brushing, preserve jawbone health, and can last a lifetime with proper care.
How often should I get my teeth professionally cleaned? +
We recommend professional teeth cleaning every 6 months for most patients. However, if you have gum disease, heavy plaque buildup, or other oral health concerns, you may benefit from more frequent cleanings (every 3-4 months). Regular cleanings prevent cavities, gum disease, and keep your smile bright and healthy.
